# Take one prompt outside

No phone or app is required once you have chosen. Print or memorize one prompt. All options depend on your own safety and access check. If conditions are unsafe, skip outdoors. No outcomes or health benefits are promised.

## Sensory pause · about 5 minutes
Sit or stand at a familiar accessible outdoor spot. Notice three colors, two sounds and one texture you can see without touching anything. No need to finish a checklist.

## Nature noticing · about 5 minutes
Observe a plant, cloud or bird at a distance. Notice what changes and what stays still. Do not taste or collect plants, approach wildlife, or identify species for safety decisions.

## Gentle wander · up to 15 minutes
Walk or roll along a familiar accessible route. Pick an easy turnaround point. Notice one detail you usually pass by. Return early if conditions or energy change.

## Familiar park loop · up to 30 minutes
Use a familiar marked accessible route, stay near an exit, and skip unsafe sections. Notice light, shade or seasonal changes without leaving the route.

## Indoor fallback
Staying inside is a valid choice. If an outdoor spot is unsafe or inaccessible, do not let a recommendation overrule that. Reassess later rather than forcing a break.
